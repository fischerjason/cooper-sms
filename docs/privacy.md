# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 26, 2026**

Jason Fischer uses SMS messaging to send personal informational messages to individuals who have explicitly consented to receive them.

## Information Collected

We may collect and process the mobile phone number of an authorized recipient and the contents of SMS messages sent or received.

This information is used to provide SMS communications such as notifications, reminders, alerts, status updates, and related informational messages.

## SMS Data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to transmit, deliver, secure, or operate the messaging service, such as telecommunications and technology providers.

## Messaging Consent

SMS messages are sent only to individuals who have explicitly consented to receive messages from Jason Fischer.

## Data Use

Information collected through SMS messaging is used only to operate, maintain, secure, and provide the requested messaging functionality.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the contact information associated with this website.
