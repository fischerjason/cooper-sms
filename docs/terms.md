# Cooper SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Cooper is a privately operated AI assistant, automation, and notification system. The Cooper SMS program is used to send informational and event-driven messages to the Cooper owner, administrator, and other specifically authorized recipients.

Cooper is not offered as a public SMS messaging service and does not use SMS for advertising, marketing, lead generation, or third-party promotional messaging.

## SMS Program Description

Cooper may send SMS messages related to configured automations, requested tasks, monitored events, connected systems, devices, and services.

Messages may include:

- reminders and scheduled notifications;
- system, application, and integration alerts;
- device or equipment status notifications;
- monitored-condition changes;
- task completion results;
- operational status updates;
- conversational responses requested through Cooper; and
- other informational notifications specifically configured within Cooper.

Messages are generated by the Cooper system based on recipient requests, configured automations, scheduled events, monitored conditions, or system activity.

## Consent and Enrollment

SMS messaging through Cooper is available only to the owner, administrator, and other specifically authorized recipients.

Additional recipients are enrolled only after providing explicit verbal consent directly to the Cooper administrator.

Before an additional recipient's mobile number is added to Cooper, the administrator explains that Cooper is an AI assistant, automation, and notification system that may send informational and event-driven SMS messages, including reminders, alerts, task results, monitored-condition updates, and other configured notifications.

Before requesting consent, the administrator provides the following disclosures:

- Message frequency varies based on configured notifications, system activity, and recipient requests.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply HELP for assistance.
- Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
- Cooper Privacy Policy: https://fischerjason.github.io/cooper-sms/privacy.html

The administrator then asks whether the recipient agrees to receive SMS messages from Cooper.

The recipient must provide an affirmative response, such as "yes," before the mobile number is added to Cooper.

Recipients who do not provide affirmative consent are not enrolled and do not receive Cooper SMS messages.

After consent is provided and the mobile number is enrolled, Cooper sends an enrollment confirmation identifying the Cooper SMS program and providing information about message frequency, possible message and data rates, HELP and STOP instructions, and a link to the Cooper Privacy Policy.

Consent to receive Cooper SMS messages is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and is not fixed.

The number of messages a recipient receives depends on:

- scheduled reminders and notifications;
- system or device activity;
- configured monitoring rules;
- task execution;
- automation events;
- recipient interaction with Cooper; and
- other notifications that the recipient or administrator has specifically configured.

Some periods may have no messages, while periods with increased system activity or configured events may generate additional notifications.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and service plan.

Cooper does not charge a separate fee for SMS messages.

Recipients are responsible for any messaging or data charges imposed by their wireless carrier.

## Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of Cooper SMS messaging at any time by replying:

**STOP**

After a valid STOP request is received, Cooper will stop sending SMS messages to that mobile number.

The recipient may receive a final confirmation message indicating that SMS messaging has been disabled.

A recipient who has opted out will not receive additional Cooper SMS messages unless the recipient later provides consent again and SMS messaging is intentionally re-enabled.

## Help and Support

Recipients may reply:

**HELP**

for assistance with the Cooper SMS program.

HELP responses may provide information about the Cooper messaging program, opt-out instructions, or other support information.

Questions regarding Cooper SMS messaging may also be submitted through the Cooper SMS project's GitHub repository.
